Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Mobius is a free library of physics and maths books and lectures. In short: we don't ask who you are, we use no cookies, ads or tracking, and nothing leaves your device unless you choose it: a question you send to the AI assistant, if you turn it on, or a lecture video you press play on, which then comes from YouTube.

Who runs Mobius

Mobius is run by its founder and designer, Zhehat, with RAMN as its developer. You can reach us on Instagram at @mobius.sci.

What we don't collect

There are no accounts, sign-ups, ads, analytics or tracking on Mobius, and the site sets no cookies. We don't sell or share personal information.

What your browser keeps on your device

  • Your choice of light or dark mode and of language, and whether you turned on AI answers, are saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them next time.
  • Your conversation with the Mobius assistant is kept in the browser tab until you close it or press "New chat".
  • The stars you gave books and courses, and a random code your browser made for itself so that it can change its rating later.

These stay on your device and we can't see them. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting

Mobius is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles technical information such as your IP address, your browser type and the pages you open, to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. The site's fonts are served from Mobius itself, so opening a page doesn't contact Google or anyone else.

Lecture videos (YouTube)

  • The Lectures pages show courses whose videos are hosted on YouTube. Opening a lecture page loads nothing from YouTube: the list of videos and the drawn covers come from Mobius itself.
  • When you press play, that video is loaded from YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). From then on YouTube, a Google service, receives technical information such as your IP address and which video you watch, and may store data in your browser for the player, under Google's privacy policy.
  • "Watch on YouTube" and the course links open YouTube itself, outside Mobius.

The Mobius assistant and AI answers

  • By default the assistant answers inside your browser, using the site's own list of books. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
  • If you confirm that you are 18 or over and turn on AI answers, some of your questions are sent, with the last few messages of the conversation and the name of the page you're on, through our server at Cloudflare to Google's Gemini service, which writes the answer. We don't store these questions.
  • We use Gemini's free service. Under Google's terms, Google may keep what is sent, use it to provide and improve its products, and have people read it. Please don't type names, contact details or anything private. See also Google's privacy policy.
  • AI answers are not offered in the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the United Kingdom.
  • AI answers can be wrong. Check anything important in the books themselves.

Star ratings

When you rate a book or a course, the number of stars, which book or course it is, and your browser's random code are sent to our server at Cloudflare and kept there, so that everyone can see the average. The code is random and does not say who you are. To stop misuse, the server counts the ratings that come from one network address in a day, under a one-way code of the address that is deleted the next day; your IP address itself is not stored.

Feedback

The Feedback page writes your message into an email in your own email app, addressed to Mobius; nothing is sent until you send it from there. We use these emails only to reply and to improve Mobius, and we don't share them.

Children

Students of any age can use Mobius. The AI answers, however, are only for people aged 18 or over. Younger visitors can use everything else, including the assistant's own answers.

Links to other sites

Book and lecture links can lead to other websites. They have their own privacy policies, which we don't control.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: the Feedback page, or Instagram @mobius.sci.

الاستضافة

موبيوس مستضاف لدى Cloudflare. ومثل أي مستضيف للمواقع، تعالج Cloudflare معلومات تقنية مثل عنوان IP ونوع المتصفح والصفحات التي تفتحها، لتوصيل الموقع وحمايته من الهجمات. راجع سياسة الخصوصية لدى Cloudflare. خطوط الموقع تُحمَّل من موبيوس نفسه، فلا يتصل فتح الصفحة بـ Google أو بأي جهة أخرى.

فيديوهات المحاضرات (YouTube)

  • عندما تضغط تشغيل، يُحمَّل ذلك الفيديو من وضع الخصوصية المحسّن في YouTube ‏(youtube-nocookie.com). ومنذ تلك اللحظة تتلقى YouTube، وهي خدمة من Google، معلومات تقنية مثل عنوان IP والفيديو الذي تشاهده، وقد تحفظ بيانات في متصفحك للمشغّل، وفق سياسة الخصوصية لدى Google.
  • رابط «شاهد على YouTube» وروابط المقررات تفتح YouTube نفسه خارج موبيوس.

  • قد تكون إجابات الذكاء الاصطناعي خاطئة، فتحقّق من أي معلومة مهمة في الكتب نفسها.

عندما تقيّم كتاباً أو مقرراً، يُرسَل عدد النجوم واسم الكتاب أو المقرر والرمز العشوائي لمتصفحك إلى خادمنا لدى Cloudflare ويُحفظ هناك، حتى يرى الجميع المتوسط. الرمز عشوائي ولا يدل على هويتك. ولمنع إساءة الاستخدام، يعدّ الخادم التقييمات القادمة من عنوان شبكة واحد في اليوم، تحت رمز أحادي الاتجاه لذلك العنوان يُحذف في اليوم التالي، ولا يُحفظ عنوان IP نفسه.
